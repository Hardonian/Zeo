Frequently Asked Questions

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, absolutely. There are no long-term contracts for the monthly plans. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time.

Is there a free trial?

We offer a 14-day free trial on the Professional plan. No credit card is required to start your trial.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) and PayPal. For Enterprise plans, we support invoicing.

Do you offer refunds?

If you're not satisfied within the first 30 days, we'll offer a full refund, no questions asked.
