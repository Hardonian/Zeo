Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data secure and private? expand_more

Yes, we use AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. Enterprise customers can choose specific regions for data residency to comply with local regulations.

How does usage-based billing work? expand_more

Professional plans pay a base monthly fee, plus a small increment for each decision branch computed. You can set monthly caps to avoid unexpected costs.

Can I cancel or downgrade anytime? expand_more

Absolutely. If you cancel your subscription, you will retain access to your plan features until the end of your current billing cycle.

Do you offer discounts for non-profits? expand_more

We provide special pricing for academic institutions and registered non-profits. Please contact our sales team for more information.
