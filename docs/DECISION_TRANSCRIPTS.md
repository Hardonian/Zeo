# Decision Transcripts

A Decision Transcript is Zeo's deterministic execution record for one decision run.

## What it is

- Append-only and deterministic.
- Replayable from transcript inputs.
- Hash-verifiable through SHA-256 transcript hashing.
- Human-readable JSON that remains machine-verifiable.

## What it is not

- Not a blockchain.
- Not a hosted service.
- Not mutable state.
- Not probabilistic logging.

## Why Zeo produces it

Decision outputs alone do not show boundary conditions or sensitivity. A transcript preserves:

- Inputs and assumptions used by the core engine.
- Evidence provenance and cost/time/risk metadata.
- Boundary and counterfactual conditions that could change outcomes.
- Agent proposals and Zeo adjudication.

## Verify and replay

From repo root:

- `zeo --emit-transcript --json-only` to emit transcript output.
- `zeo transcript verify <file>` to validate schema/hash/invariants.
- `zeo transcript replay <file>` to re-run and assert deterministic agreement.
- `zeo transcript diff <a> <b>` to compare input/evidence changes and boundary flips.

## Difference from AI explanations

AI explanations are often post-hoc narratives. Zeo transcripts are generated by deterministic core execution and are hash-addressed artifacts that can be independently replayed and verified.
